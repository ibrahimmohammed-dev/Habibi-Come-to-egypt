Ras Mohammed National Park

One of Egypt's most beautiful natural treasures, located at the southern tip of Sinai.

About the Park

Ras Mohammed National Park is located at the southernmost tip of the Sinai Peninsula, where the Gulf of Suez and the Gulf of Aqaba meet. It was established in 1983 and was the first national park in Egypt.

The park covers around 480 km² of both land and sea. It is known for its stunning coral reefs, clear blue water, and rich wildlife. The name "Ras Mohammed" means "Head of Mohammed" in Arabic and refers to the rocky headland at the park's tip.

Marine Life

The park is home to some of the most diverse marine ecosystems in the world. Its coral reefs are among the healthiest in the Red Sea.

🪸 Coral Reefs

Over 220 species of coral can be found in the park, some of them hundreds of years old.

🐠 Tropical Fish

More than 1,000 species of fish live in these waters, including clownfish, parrotfish, and barracuda.

🦈 Sharks & Rays

The park is famous for shark diving. Shark Reef is one of the top dive sites in the Red Sea.

🐢 Sea Turtles

Green and hawksbill turtles nest on the beaches and feed along the reefs inside the park.

🌿 Mangroves

Rare mangrove forests grow along the coast and serve as an important habitat for many species.

🦅 Birds

Millions of migratory birds pass through the park each year on their way between Africa and Europe.

How to Visit

Getting There

  • Located 12 km south of Sharm El-Sheikh
  • Accessible by car or taxi (about 20 minutes)
  • Boat trips available from Sharm marina

Best Time to Visit

  • Open all year round
  • Best diving: March to November
  • Winter is good for birdwatching and hiking

Important Info

  • Entry fee is required
  • Bring your passport or ID
  • Do not touch or feed the marine life
  • Park hours: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
