Information about the Pyramids

The Pyramids of Giza are among the most famous ancient monuments in the world and are located near Cairo, Egypt. They were built more than 4,500 years ago as tombs for the pharaohs of the Old Kingdom, especially the Great Pyramid of Khufu, which is the largest one. The pyramids were constructed using huge stone blocks and show the high level of engineering and organization achieved by ancient Egyptians. The Great Pyramid is the only remaining wonder of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
